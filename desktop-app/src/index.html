Let's get Recruit Desk ready

Recruit Desk checks a few things on this computer before the assistants can work.

    Logs

    What the app wrote about itself, from everyone in your company: events and errors, how each run went, and sign-ins. They hold no search text, no candidates and no contact details. Only admins can read them, and lines older than 30 days are removed.

    Search history

    People and access

    Invite people, change who is an admin, and remove people from your company.

    Invite someone

    An invitation is a code you hand to the person (nothing is emailed). It works once, only for that email address, and for a limited time. You can show it again from the waiting invitations below.

      About Recruit Desk

      A new version is installed on its own when no assistant is running and nothing is typed into a run form. You see a 15 second countdown first and can press Later.

      Built by kaustubh.s.v

      Three AI research assistants for recruiters: one finds candidates for a job, one checks a company's open jobs, and one researches a company as an account (what it builds, offices, funding, leaders and open engineering roles, each with a source). Results are saved to Excel files that this app shows but never changes.

      Technical details

      The allowance applies to this app only. Claude Code keeps its normal permission mode; each assistant's own settings decide what it may do.

      Export for a CRM

      The file is saved in the assistant's exports folder, which opens when it is ready. Blank cells stay blank.
